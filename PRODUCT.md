# Auravine — Product

## What it is

Auravine is an AI-first software development company in Dhaka, Bangladesh. This repository is its marketing website. The site's job is to make local and international business owners trust Auravine enough to book a free consultation.

## Who it is for

- Owners and managers of businesses in Bangladesh: clinics and hospitals, retailers, garment buying houses, factories, phone and electronics shops, logistics and mobility operators.
- International companies looking for an AI and software engineering partner in South Asia.

They are not engineers. They judge by whether Auravine understands their operations and shows work that looks real.

## What Auravine sells

- AI voice agents for customer calls, in Bangla and English
- Custom chatbots for websites, WhatsApp, and Messenger, answering from the client's own documents
- Computer vision: face recognition attendance, quality inspection
- Business analytics and demand forecasting
- Document intelligence with cited answers
- Custom web and mobile software around the AI
