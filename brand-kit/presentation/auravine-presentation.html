What we heard

The brief

An AI-first software company in Dhaka, Bangladesh that builds voice agents, chatbots, computer vision, analytics and custom software for non-technical business owners (clinics, retail, garment, logistics). The identity must make those owners trust Auravine to build their product: trustworthy, intelligent, growing, hand-built.

trustworthyintelligentwelcominggrowing

Success: reads as an A at 16 px, survives one colour and reversal, avoids AI clichés (sparkles, brains, node networks) and the eco-leaf cliché, and fits the existing emerald and Inter system.

Auravine — final identity2
Final design

Doorway A

A solid A that frames an arched doorway, with one small emerald presence inside: a welcome and an AI core in a single shape.

  • The A silhouette (true 60 degree edges) is the strongest letter cue and survives 16 px
  • The doorway says welcome and trust to non-technical owners; the dot is the aura, the intelligence in the room
  • One accent colour only: the dot is the brand emerald, the rest is ink, so it extends cleanly into the existing site
